SubWatch privacy policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

The short version

SubWatch keeps track of your subscriptions and free trials on your iPhone. Everything you add stays on your phone. SubWatch has no account and sends none of your information anywhere.

What we don't collect

  • No account, sign-up, name or email.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • The subscriptions, prices and dates you enter are never uploaded or sent off your device.

Your data stays on your device

The subscriptions and free trials you add — their names, prices and renewal dates — are stored only on your iPhone. They are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.

Reminders

SubWatch can remind you before a free trial ends or a subscription renews. These are local notifications scheduled on your own device. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

SubWatch is a paid app, bought once through Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

SubWatch does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
