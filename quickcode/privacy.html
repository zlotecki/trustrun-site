QuickCode privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026 (Pro)

The short version

QuickCode makes QR codes on your iPhone. What you type and the QR codes you make never leave your phone unless you share them yourself.

What we don't collect

  • No account, sign-up, name or email.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • The text and links you turn into QR codes are not sent anywhere.

History and logos (QuickCode Pro)

Your saved QR history and any logo you add are kept only on your iPhone. They are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

QuickCode Pro is bought through Apple. We never see your payment details.

Saving to Photos

If you tap Save Image, iOS asks for permission to add the picture to your photo library. QuickCode can only add photos; it cannot see or read your library.

Crash reports

If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a short report to our server: the app's name and version, the error message and the time. It does not include anything you typed, your QR codes, your name or any way to identify you. Reports are deleted automatically after 30 days.

Children

QuickCode does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
