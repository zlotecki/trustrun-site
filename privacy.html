Privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

The short version

Trust Run runs on your own Mac. It does not upload your files, passwords, browsing or activity to us or anyone else.

What stays on your Mac

  • A list of programs you chose to always trust.
  • A history of the warnings Trust Run showed you.

Both are small files in a folder in your home directory. You can delete them any time.

What Trust Run looks at

To warn you, Trust Run checks which programs are running, how much processor they use, which sensitive files they open, and which servers they connect to. This is done on your Mac and is not sent to us.

What we do receive

  • Purchases: handled by our payment provider, which collects your payment details and email. We never see your card number.
  • Update checks: the app may contact our server to ask whether a new version exists. This request does not include your files or activity.
